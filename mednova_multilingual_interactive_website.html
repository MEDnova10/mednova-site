About Us

MedNova is an Omani startup developing AI-powered wearable solutions for physical rehabilitation. Our smart glove is designed to help patients recover hand mobility with personalized therapy.

The Product

Our smart glove combines artificial intelligence, motion sensors, and a mobile app to deliver customized rehabilitation programs. It is ideal for stroke survivors, elderly patients, and individuals with muscular disorders.

The glove includes Bluetooth connectivity, rechargeable batteries, and real-time app integration with therapist dashboards. It is lightweight, ergonomic, and built with patient comfort in mind.

Who It's For

  • Stroke patients
  • Elderly individuals
  • People with muscular atrophy
  • Rehabilitation centers and hospitals
